# Code of Conduct

Jarrow adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The full text at that link is the binding version, including its enforcement guidelines. This page is a short summary and two project-specific additions.

## The short version

- Treat everyone in this project with respect, whatever their background, identity, experience or opinion.
- Disagree about code and ideas, not about people. Assume good faith.
- No harassment, insults, sexual content, threats, or publishing someone's private information.
- Accept correction gracefully, and own your mistakes.

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, Discussions, and any other space where you represent Jarrow.

## Be kind to the ecosystem

Jarrow lives inside a community it did not build. In every public word about the project:

- Be polite to the maintainers of Sinytra Connector, Fabric and NeoForge. They built the ecosystem this project depends on, and their work is the reason any of this is possible.
- Do not file issues upstream about Jarrow's output. If a converted mod breaks, that is Jarrow's bug, reported here.
- Credit Connector where its public issue tracker informed a rule. Rule tables cite their sources.
- Mod authors decide what happens to their work. Respect an opt-out, a "do not port" request or a license, even when the law might allow more.

## Reporting a problem

To report a conduct problem, open a private report to the maintainer at https://github.com/MosheByte. A dedicated conduct address will be added when the project has its own domain.

Do not use the security advisory form for conduct reports. That form is for vulnerabilities only.

Reports are handled privately. The maintainer follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban, depending on what happened.
